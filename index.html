Terms of Service

yt-ai-pipeline is a local automation pipeline built and run by a single individual to produce and publish short-form video content to that individual's own social media accounts. It is not distributed, sold, or made available to any other user, and no one other than the operator authenticates against it.

What it does

The pipeline generates video scripts, narration, and imagery locally, assembles them into finished videos, and - where a platform's API and the operator's authorization allow it - publishes those videos directly to the operator's own channel or account.

Scope of use

  • Used solely by the operator, on the operator's own machine.
  • Publishes only to accounts the operator owns and has personally authorized via each platform's official OAuth flow.
  • Makes no claims of fitness for any purpose beyond this personal use.

Changes

Since this is a personal tool rather than a distributed product, these terms change whenever the pipeline's own use changes. The date at the top of this page reflects the last revision.

Privacy Policy

This pipeline does not collect, process, or share data belonging to anyone other than its operator, and it has no other users.

What is stored, and where

OAuth access & refresh tokens Stored locally on the operator's own machine, used only to publish to the operator's own accounts
video.publish (TikTok) Publishes a video the operator generated to the operator's own TikTok profile
youtube.upload (YouTube) Uploads a video the operator generated to the operator's own YouTube channel
instagram_business_content_publish (Instagram) Publishes a video the operator generated to the operator's own Instagram account

What is never collected

  • No data from any third party or platform viewer.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to any other party.

Deleting data

Because everything lives on the operator's own machine, deleting the stored token files removes all locally held credentials. Revoking access from within TikTok's, YouTube's, or Meta's own account settings additionally invalidates them on the platform side.
